What You Get As Part Of The Team

We have built Psykolog.no around one idea: that psychologists should spend their time on patients, not on marketing and administration.

You choose how many clients you accept, which days, and whether you want to scale up or down over time.

Visibility, marketing and new orders are our responsibility. You don't have to build your own customer base from scratch.

Payment without reminders. We handle invoicing and payment from the client.

Receive clients digitally from wherever you want in the country, or physically at our clinic in Oslo or Ski.

Participate in regular peer guidance and professional meetings with other psychologists and psychiatrists in the network and don't be alone with the difficult cases.

Access to internal professional conferences and professional updates, in addition to experience with a wide range of issues.
