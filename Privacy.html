At Psykolog.no, we want psychologist conversations over video to be at least as safe and secure as conversations at the clinic. Under the GDPR regulations, we are obliged to inform you about how we process personal data. You can read more about this in this privacy statement.

What is Recorded?

According to the Health Personnel Act and the Patient and User Rights Act, essential information from conversations and relevant communication with a psychologist must be recorded. For your own safety, this may also include identifiable information, such as your residential address, personal identification number, and contact information for your GP or next of kin.

This sensitive information is stored securely, using encryption technology and two-factor login in line with Norwegian data and privacy protection requirements and EU/EEA requirements through GDPR.

As long as there is no danger to the life or health of you or others, and you do not consent, your psychologist is legally bound by confidentiality towards your GP, relatives, and other public authorities.

What personal data do we process, and for what purpose?

According to the Health Personnel Act and the Patient and User Rights Act, essential information from conversations and relevant communication with a psychologist must be recorded. For your own safety, this may also include identifiable information, such as your residential address, personal identification number, and contact information for your GP or next of kin.

We process your personal data for one purpose: to provide you with psychological treatment. Recording this information is necessary to provide healthcare services. We only collect, process, and store information that is relevant and necessary for this purpose.

For more information, see the regulations on patient records at lovdata.no.

Disclosure of personal data

We do not share your personal information with other parties, and it is not released to third parties. We only disclose personal information to the extent required by law. Examples of this may be if your psychologist suspects that you may be a danger to yourself or others, or if the psychologist's reporting obligation is triggered because there is suspicion that a child under the age of 18 is being seriously neglected.

Secure storage of personal data

Psykolog.no uses booking system from makeplans.com and the journal system from Aspit.no Psykbase for processing personal data. Data processing agreements have been entered into with the following suppliers: Aspit AS (Aspit.no)

Our video consultation solution is fully encrypted and meets the requirements for secure online conversation solutions. No audio, images or video from the conversation is stored.

Should a security breach occur that poses a high risk to you, we will notify you as soon as possible.

Deletion of information

Records are deleted when, for reasons of health care, they are no longer deemed necessary. If the record information is not to be preserved according to the Archives Act or other legislation, it is normally deleted 10 years after the last record entry.

Correcting incorrect or incomplete information in the medical record can be done relatively easily. However, deleting medical record information is strictly regulated in Section 43 of the Health Personnel Act and requires that certain conditions are met; the medical record cannot be deleted just because you want it to be. Contact us if you believe your medical record contains errors, and we will guide you further.

Your rights

  • Right to request access to and receive a copy of your records.
  • Right to request corrections to your data (with a 30-day response timeframe).
  • Right to request deletion where we no longer have a legal basis to retain data.
  • Right to restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability for information you have provided.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the State Administrator (Statsforvalteren) or the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).
