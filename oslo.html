Do You Need a Psychologist in
Oslo?

Finding the right psychological help in Oslo should not be complicated. At Psykolog.no you can book directly without waiting for a GP referral and without joining a long public queue.

We offer psychological help for adults, individuals, couples, working professionals, and students. Sessions are held either in person at our Oslo clinic or via secure video from your home, office, or any private space.

At Psykolog.no, most patients are seen within 1 to 3 working days. You choose your psychologist. You choose your format. Everything you discussed in your session is confidential. Our psychologists are bound by Norwegian healthcare law and by professional standards set by the Norwegian Psychological Association.
