Choose Your Session Format

Individual Therapy

Meet your psychologist at the clinic. A good fit if you prefer talking in a quiet, dedicated room, or if your assessment requires an in-person visit.

Video Consultation

Talk to your psychologist over a secure video connection from wherever you are. You'll receive a link by SMS shortly before your session. Available nationwide.

Phone Consultation

For those who prefer a conversation without video or who don't have a stable internet connection. Same clinical quality as video and in-person sessions.

What's Included In The Price?

The price covers a full session with an authorized psychologist, secure in an approved clinical system, and, where needed, a short written summary or documentation for your employer, on request. There are no separate booking fees, platform fees, or hidden add ons.

Payment

You pay after the lesson. You will receive a payment link via SMS and email, where you can choose Vipps, card or invoice.

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 48 hours (two business days) before the lesson. Late cancellations or no-shows will be invoiced in full.

Change Psychologist

You can change psychologists at any time, at no extra cost. What matters most to us is that you feel comfortable with the person you're talking to.
