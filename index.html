✳Who is a psychologist?

A licensed professional, trained to help you understand what is happening.

A psychologist is a person with a master's degree in psychology and authorisation from the Norwegian Directorate of Health. Specialising in the assessment and treatment of mental disorders through talk therapy. Such as cognitive therapy (CBT), EMDR, and psychodynamic therapy. Unlike a psychiatrist, a psychologist cannot prescribe medication.

Psychotherapies

We offer a wide range of psychotherapies and therapy methods, tailored to your needs.

ISTDP Emotion-Focused Therapy (EFT) Exposure therapy Prolonged Exposure Body-oriented psychotherapy Trauma treatment (specialised) Mindfulness ADHD guidance

CBT / KAT

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

CBT helps you identify unhelpful thinking patterns and behaviours that contribute to anxiety, depression, stress, and other mental health conditions.

Psychodynamic

Psychodynamic Therapy

This approach explores how past experiences and patterns influence your current emotions, behaviors, and relationships.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitisation

Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR) helps the brain process traumatic memories so they become less distressing over time.

MCT

Metacognitive Therapy

MCT helps reduce excessive worrying and overthinking by changing how you respond to your thoughts instead of changing the thoughts themselves.
