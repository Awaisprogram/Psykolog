About Psykolog.no

We are a Norwegian mental health platform built to make it easier for people across Norway to find and book an authorised psychologist. Fast, without a referral, and without a long wait.

Mental Health Help For Everyone

At psykolog.no, we believe that mental health is the foundation of a good life. When you are struggling, whether with anxiety, depression, sleep problems, stress, or relationship difficulties. You deserve access to qualified, professional help.

We built psykolog.no to make specialized psychological treatment accessible to everyone. We connect you with an authorised psychologist who can help. We offer both in- person consultations at our clinics in Oslo and Ski and video consultations for patients across all of Norway. No referral from your GP is needed.

What We Treat

Our psychologists and specialists treat a wide range of mental health conditions using evidence-based methods.

Depression

Low mood, loss of interest, fatigue, and negative thinking. CBT and other therapies provide strong, lasting relief.

Anxiety & social anxiety

General anxiety, social anxiety disorder, panic disorder, phobias, and performance anxiety.

Sleep problems

Insomnia, difficulty falling asleep, waking at night. CBT-I is the gold-standard treatment.

Anger management

Anger outbursts, irritability, and difficulty controlling reactions. CBT and practical skills help.

ADHD assessment

Adult ADHD screening, diagnosis, and treatment planning in collaboration with specialist doctors.

Trauma & PTSD

Complex and single-event trauma, PTSD, and stress-related conditions using EMDR and CBT.

Personality disorders

Borderline, avoidant, narcissistic, and other personality disorders using DBT, MBT, and schema therapy.

Stress & burnout

Work-related stress, chronic burnout, and exhaustion syndrome with practical recovery programs.

Workplace mental health

Corporate psychology services for employers who want to support their employees' mental wellbeing.

Why Choose Psykolog.no?

Here is what makes psykolog.no different.

You do not need to see your GP first. Book directly with a psychologist, without any waiting or administrative hurdles.

Every psychologist and psychiatrist in our network is authorised by Helsedirektoratet and has trained at recognized Norwegian hospitals.

Typical waiting time is 1 to 3 working days. When you are struggling, you should not have to wait months for help.
